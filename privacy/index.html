Privacy Policy

Same Patient, by Yun Pai. Effective 14 November 2026.

The short version

Same Patient does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off: which journeys you have finished, your best quiz score, your language choice and whether you have read the opening notice. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Links to other websites

The app links to public sources, such as government and health insurer websites. Tapping a link opens it in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email yunpai1012@gmail.com.

隱私權政策（繁體中文）

《同病不同命》不蒐集、不儲存也不分享任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具，也不追蹤使用者。

App 只會在你的 iPhone 上儲存少量設定，例如已完成的旅程、小測驗最佳成績、語言選擇，以及是否已閱讀開場聲明。這些資料不會離開你的裝置，刪除 App 時也會一併刪除。

App 內的資料來源連結會在瀏覽器中開啟，這些網站各有其隱私權政策。如有疑問，請寄信至 yunpai1012@gmail.com。
