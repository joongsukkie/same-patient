Same Patient

An educational pixel-art game by Yun Pai. One patient, ten health systems.

Get in touch

Found a bug, spotted an outdated fact, or have an idea? Email yunpai1012@gmail.com. Corrections to facts are especially welcome, and a link to the source helps.

Common questions

How do I switch language?
Tap the language button at the top right of the title screen. The app supports English and Traditional Chinese.

How do I reset my progress?
Delete the app and install it again. Progress is stored only on your device.

Is this medical advice?
No. Same Patient is an educational simulation about how health systems are organised. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment. If you need care, please talk to a doctor or your local health service.

Where do the facts come from?
Every fact links to a public source inside the app. Open About & Sources to see the full list.

Privacy
